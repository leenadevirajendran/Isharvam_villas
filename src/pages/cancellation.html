Cancellation Policy

Last updated: November 2026. Our team will always confirm the exact terms for your dates before you pay.

Cancellation schedule

When you cancelRefund
14 days or more before check-in90% of amount paid
Between 7 and 14 days before check-in50% of amount paid
Fewer than 7 days before check-inNo refund
No-showNo refund

Changing dates

Dates can usually be changed subject to availability. Please message us as early as you can so we can help.

How to cancel

Message the team on WhatsApp with your booking reference, or use the cancel option under My bookings.

Refunds

Eligible refunds are returned to the original payment method. Our team will confirm the timeline when you cancel.
