Using this website

The site lets you browse villas and send booking requests. A request is not a confirmed booking.

Bookings and confirmation

A stay is confirmed only after the Isharvam team confirms it with you, typically over WhatsApp. Dates are held for you while payment is being arranged, for a period our team will confirm with you.

Payment

Payment is not taken on this website. It is arranged directly with the team. Accepted methods, the advance amount and the balance due date are confirmed with you in your booking conversation.
