Information we collect

When you send a booking request or contact us we may collect your name, mobile number, email address, travel dates, group size and any notes you provide. When you message us on WhatsApp, that conversation is subject to WhatsApp's own terms and privacy policy.

How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry and manage your booking request.
  • To arrange payment and confirm your stay with you.
  • To keep records required by law: [to be confirmed].

Sharing

We share details only with the people who help us run your stay, such as our property teams and payment partners, and where the law requires it. We never sell your personal information.

Retention and your rights

We keep booking records only as long as needed for your stay and for legal and accounting purposes. You can ask us at any time to access, correct or delete your information by writing to isharvam@gmail.com.

Cookies

This website uses only the small amount of browser storage needed to remember your wishlist, dates and booking requests. We do not use advertising cookies.
